What the optimizer learned

  • →Charge into the solar surplus. Battery action is positive through the early afternoon, when PV covers the load and the TOU price is still low.
  • →Discharge into the peaks. 07:00–09:00 and 18:00–21:00 are where stored energy is worth most.
  • →Pre-cool instead of paying. Setpoints drop before the expensive window so the building coasts through it.
  • →Lighting is nearly free. Dimming saves little in this building — hence the wide 0–1 spread between solutions.
The comfort constraint is what stops the optimizer from simply switching everything off: PPD must stay low, so the cheapest plan still holds a usable setpoint.
