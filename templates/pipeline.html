The pipeline, end to end

Raw sensors in, control schedule out — five stages, no manual steps.

Python · pandas · XGBoost · NumPy
1
Collect & clean

HouseZero hourly sensors are merged with outdoor weather, ASHRAE comfort data and CU-BEMS loads; duplicates dropped, gaps forward-filled, units normalised.

2
Engineer features

Lags (1 h → 168 h), rolling means and standard deviations, cyclical hour/month/day-of-week encoding, an occupancy proxy from CO₂, PMV/PPD from the Fanger equation, and the TOU tariff for the timestamp.

3
Train the forecaster

{{ metrics.type }} on a chronological 70 / 15 / 15 split, MinMax scaling fitted on train only, early stopping against the validation fold.

4
Optimize the day

NSGA-II evolves 72 genes (setpoint, lighting, battery × 24 h) against three objectives — cost, energy, mean PPD — keeping a non-dominated front instead of a single weighted answer.

5
Serve the result

This site renders the stored artefacts and can re-run the whole optimizer on an uploaded CSV through POST /api/run.

Known limitations

  • !One building only — HouseZero. Multi-building validation is still open.
  • !Tariff is a static TOU schedule; real-time pricing would change the battery policy hour by hour.
  • !Plant model is simplified: no thermal mass, humidity or ramp-rate dynamics.
  • !PMV assumes fixed metabolic rate (1.2 met) and clothing (0.5 clo).
  • !No demand-charge or grid-signal (demand response) objective yet.
